MONEY TRACKER v1.0.0 · Android

Every cent, tracked by hand — not by your bank.

A manual-entry finance app: wallets, credit cards, budgets and reports, all kept on your phone. No bank login, no cloud account, no ads.

Download the APK v1.0.0 · Android 8.0+

Direct install, no accounts required.

What it does

  • ✓

    Wallets and credit cards, one true total

    Track cash, bank accounts, and cards side by side. Everything rolls up into a single balance you can trust.

  • ✓

    Due-date reminders that actually fire

    Exact-alarm scheduling for credit card due dates, with a status check for the permissions that make notifications reliable.

  • ✓

    Budgets, savings goals, and reports

    Per-category budgets, per-wallet savings goals, and a dashboard with spending charts and custom date ranges.

  • ✓

    PIN and fingerprint lock

    The app locks on its own. Amounts stay masked by default until you tap to reveal them.

  • ✓

    Your data stays yours

    CSV export and import for backups, plus optional local and Google Drive backup. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

  • ✓

    English and Español

    Full translation, switchable in Settings independent of your phone's system language.
